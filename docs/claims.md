# First Light Hunt claims

From dawn pastoral photographs and the style lock, written before the loop.

1. **Two temperatures.** Gold key on grass and hide; cool blue in shade and sky-facing planes.
2. **Ground couples.** Meadow and sand take the sun. A dark floating carpet fails.
3. **Occupied near field.** Grass tufts, fences or bushes sit in the lower third on foot and at gallop.
4. **Hero scale.** The horse is large in frame when mounted; the hunter is not a speck on a toy.

A metric that scores a bald green plane equal to a dressed meadow is rejected.
